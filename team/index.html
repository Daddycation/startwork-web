How to set up your team

  1. One person sets up StartWork: Jira address, tiles for your tickets, rounding.
  2. Settings → Team → “Share team template” creates a link – for your team chat, say.
  3. Whoever opens the link sees what will be applied, confirms, and enters their own token. Done.

What’s in the link – and what isn’t

In it: Jira address, tiles (ticket, title, customer, color), rounding, and whether AI polishing is allowed.

Not in it: token, email, AI key, worklogs. If a link points to a different Jira address than before, StartWork deletes the stored token – it never goes to a foreign address.

For IT: deploy with device management

Buy licenses in bulk in Apple Business under “Apps and Books” and assign them to devices or people through your device management (MDM).

With managed app configuration you can preset two values for StartWork:

vorlage
Link to a team template (string). StartWork applies it at launch without asking – once per change; after that, people may adjust their tiles.
kiAus
true (boolean) turns AI polishing off. It can’t be switched on in the app, and nothing is sent to Anthropic.
